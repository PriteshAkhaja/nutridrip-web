Home · Safety and testing

Tested before you're treated. Watched while you're treated.

IV nutrient therapy is a medical procedure. This is exactly how we make it a safe one, step by step.

Six steps, every drip, no exceptions

  1. Online assessmentA scoring questionnaire maps your answers to a suggested drip and flags anything the doctor needs to know.
  2. Pre-drip testsCBP, RFT and LFT for everyone. G6PD before glutathione or high-dose vitamin C. B12, vitamin D, TSH, HbA1c or iron studies where indicated.
  3. Doctor consult and prescriptionA NutriDrip doctor reviews your score and results, screens for contraindications and prescribes. Injectables are prescription medicines.
  4. Compounded to protocolPrepared in the prep room only, strictly to the written protocol for that drip. No substitution, dose change or off-protocol mixing.
  5. Infusion over 45 to 60 minutesAseptic technique, two-point identification, the correct rate, and emergency drugs at the chair.
  6. Follow-upObservation, aftercare notes and a follow-up call.

Every pre-drip test, and why it matters

The baseline panel is mandatory before a first drip. The others are added for specific drips or specific people.

Iron studiesWhen the doctor needs to check iron stores.
Allergy historyIncluding fish and shellfish, before any collagen drip.

Protocol rules we never bend

From our written protocol book: how to mix safely, which drugs interact, and how each drip is given.

  • Glutathione is never parked in the bagIt is given by slow IV push within 10 minutes of reconstitution, or in 100 mL saline over about 12 minutes.
  • Everything else goes in a 500 mL bagInfused over 45 to 60 minutes. A 500 mL bag means more hydration and less chance of interaction.
  • Ferric carboxymaltose runs on its own lineIn 0.9% saline only, never mixed with other components.

At the chair, every session

  • Emergency drugs and an anaphylaxis kitAt the chair, not merely somewhere on the premises, with a defined escalation and hospital-referral pathway.
  • Screening flagsG6PD deficiency, kidney impairment, pregnancy, thyroid status, allergy history (including fish and shellfish for collagen drips) and current medicines are built into the assessment.
  • Consent and recordsWritten informed consent before the first session. Assessment, prescription, batch numbers, administration and observation are recorded for every session.
  • Sharps and clinical wasteSegregated and disposed of through an authorised biomedical-waste operator, under the Biomedical Waste Management Rules, 2016.
  • Your dataHealth data from the assessment is handled under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: explicit consent, purpose limitation, secure storage.
