The protocol comes with the product

Every kit is built for a protocol from our written protocol book, and these rules travel with it.

  • Glutathione is never parked in the bagIt is given by slow IV push within 10 minutes of reconstitution, or in 100 mL saline over about 12 minutes.
  • Everything else goes in a 500 mL bagInfused over 45 to 60 minutes. A 500 mL bag means more hydration and less chance of interaction.
  • Ferric carboxymaltose runs on its own lineIn 0.9% saline only, never mixed with other components.
