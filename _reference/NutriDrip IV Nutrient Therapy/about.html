Home · About the doctor

Dr. Manoj Kumar Bhoomigari

Physician and medical educator. Founder of NutriDrip.

Dr. Manoj founded NutriDrip in Telangana in 2020. That year, before any COVID medicine existed, his team gave IV vitamins, minerals and antioxidants to 776 COVID-positive patients.

The years since went into a written protocol book: how to mix vitamins and minerals safely, which drugs interact, and how each drip should be given. It is the book every NutriDrip drip is prepared from, and the one we train other clinics' nurses on.

Today NutriDrip is a team of four doctors. It has delivered more than 6,000 drips, and started India's first drip distribution business.
