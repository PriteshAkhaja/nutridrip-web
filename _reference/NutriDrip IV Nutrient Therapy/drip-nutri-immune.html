What's inside, and why

Each ingredient, at the level of what the nutrient is. Your doctor sets the doses for you.

  1. Vitamin C

    In the 500 mL bag

    An antioxidant vitamin. The body also needs it to make collagen.

  2. Zinc

    In the 500 mL bag

    A mineral involved in normal immune function and skin.

  3. NAC

    In the 500 mL bag

    A building block the body uses to make its own glutathione.

  4. Vitamin B12

    In the 500 mL bag

    Needed to make red blood cells and for normal nerve function.

  5. B-complex

    In the 500 mL bag

    B1, B2, B3, B5 and B6, which help the body turn food into energy.

  6. Magnesium

    In the 500 mL bag

    A mineral used in muscle and nerve function and energy metabolism.

  7. Calcium

    In the 500 mL bag

    A mineral used in muscle contraction, nerve signalling and bone.

Questions about Nutri-Immune

Who decides if Nutri-Immune is right for me?

A NutriDrip doctor, after your online assessment, your pre-drip tests and a consultation. If your tests or consult say it isn't right for you, you won't be given it.

How many sessions will I need?

The standard course is six sessions, one a week. Single sessions are available. The doctor suggests what fits you.

How soon will I notice a difference?

It varies from person to person, and some people notice nothing. We don't promise a result.
